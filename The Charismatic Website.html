How to Smell Nice

Personal hygiene and fragrance mastery for lasting impressions

Daily Hygiene Routine

  • Shower daily with antibacterial soap
  • Use antiperspirant, not just deodorant
  • Brush teeth twice daily and floss regularly
  • Maintain clean, trimmed nails
  • Wear clean clothes made of breathable fabrics

Fragrance Mastery

  • Apply cologne to pulse points (wrists, neck, chest)
  • The "less is more" rule for fragrance application
  • Choose scents that complement your natural chemistry
  • Seasonal fragrance selection (lighter in summer, warmer in winter)
  • Layer scents with matching shower gel and lotion

How to Study Effectively

Proven techniques to maximize learning and retention

Study Techniques

  • Active recall - test yourself instead of rereading
  • Spaced repetition for long-term retention
  • Pomodoro technique (25 min study, 5 min break)
  • Feynman technique - teach what you learn
  • Create mind maps for complex topics

Optimal Study Environment

  • Dedicated, distraction-free study space
  • Natural lighting whenever possible
  • Background music without lyrics (classical, lo-fi)
  • Keep your space organized and clutter-free
  • Ergonomics - comfortable chair at proper height

Language Learning Strategies

  • Start with the 100 most common words
  • Practice daily for at least 20 minutes
  • Use spaced repetition software (Anki)
  • Focus on comprehensible input first
  • Find a language exchange partner
  • Immerse yourself with media in target language
